About

I'm Thanos Papangelis — a builder, founder, and investor based in Greece.

I co-founded Epignosis and spent over 16 years as its CEO, where our team built learning tools — eFront, TalentLMS, TalentCards, and TalentHR — now trusted by tens of thousands of organizations around the world. Today I stay involved as a board member and advisor, and I angel-invest in a handful of startups I believe in.

I have a PhD in Computer Science, and I've been writing software or leading development teams for most of my life. Lately I've been captivated by how AI is reshaping the way products get built — how one person with good taste and the right tools can now do what used to take a whole team. That’s how I ended up building Polis, a city builder that runs in a browser tab, and pixelagora.com, a hub for the strategy and world-building games I actually play.

This is where I write about how AI is transforming our world — the way we work, build, and live. Some of it is about software and organisations, some about bigger questions, but the thread is always the same: what changes when intelligence becomes cheap. You can find me on LinkedIn.
